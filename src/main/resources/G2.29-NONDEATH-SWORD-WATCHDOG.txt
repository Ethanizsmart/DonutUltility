G2.29 NON-DEATH SWORD WATCHDOG
- Optional GUI toggle under Death Recovery: Auto Buy Sword After 30s Missing (default OFF)
- While alive, continuously missing any sword for 30s arms the proven G2.28 AH sword purchase flow.
- Death recovery has priority and cancels the non-death watchdog owner.
- If a sword returns before purchase completes, AH is cancelled to avoid duplicate purchase.
- FarmCycle and Protected Auto-Use are blocked while watchdog-owned AH recovery is active.
- Toggle persists in config/autoominous-missing-sword.properties.
- G2.28 AH exact-Yes + pre-click atomic guard classes are otherwise unchanged.
