G2.18 SAFETY RECONNECT + HUD CLEANUP + BOUNTY TOTAL
- Foreign-player safety disconnect now pauses Auto Reconnect for the current safety disconnect.
- Auto Reconnect remains configured ON but is suppressed until the user manually reconnects; it then resumes for future network/time-out disconnects.
- Auto Reconnect HUD shows PAUSED while the safety interlock is active.
- Stats HUD no longer fakes bold by drawing text twice with a 1px offset; uses normal vanilla font rendering.
- Bounty HUD adds target, cumulative confirmed bounty total, last confirmed bounty, and colored status/countdown.
- Bounty total increments only from confirmed server SUCCESS messages.
